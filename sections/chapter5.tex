\chapter{Personal Area Networks \& Device-to-Device Communication}
\label{sec:chapter5}

This chapter focuses on short-range wireless communication paradigms, including personal area networks (PANs) and direct device-to-device (D2D) communications.

\section{Overview of Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Arun George:} Protocol designs and optimization for D2D resource sharing, routing, and interference management.
\end{itemize}

\section{D2D Underlay/Overlay Architectures}
We discuss D2D communication mechanisms where peer devices communicate directly without routing traffic through an Access Point or Base Station, discussing spectrum reuse options, power control, and proximity discovery.
