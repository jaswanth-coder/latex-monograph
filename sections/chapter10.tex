\chapter{Cross-Layer Design, Full-Duplex, and SIC}
\label{sec:chapter10}

This chapter details cross-layer protocol architectures that bridge the physical (PHY) and medium access control (MAC) layers to maximize spectral efficiency.

\section{Overview of Contributions}
Contributions from researchers under the Mobile Computing Lab include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Arun I B:} Multi-packet reception (MPR) MAC layer design.
    \item \textbf{Ankit:} Successive Interference Cancellation (SIC) and Full-Duplex MAC protocol evaluation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Joint PHY-MAC Enhancements}
We discuss full-duplex communication mechanisms, where nodes transmit and receive simultaneously on the same channel, alongside Successive Interference Cancellation (SIC) techniques to resolve concurrent packet collisions.
