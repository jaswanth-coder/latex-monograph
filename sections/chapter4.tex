\chapter{5G Mobile Networks}
\label{sec:chapter4}

This chapter investigates developments in fifth-generation (5G) mobile network protocols and architecture, with an emphasis on MAC-layer resource management, scheduling, and beam management.

\section{Overview of Contributions}
Contributions from researchers under the Mobile Computing Lab include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Lokesh:} 5G MAC scheduling and resource block allocation.
    \item \textbf{Sagar:} Beamforming optimization and cell association.
    \item \textbf{Ujjwal:} Ultra-Reliable Low-Latency Communication (URLLC) protocols.
    \item \textbf{Santhosh Kumar:} Network slicing and spectrum optimization.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary of 5G Performance Enhancements}
Detailed analyses of student research papers will highlight novel scheduler designs, latency reduction techniques, and capacity improvements in sub-6 GHz and millimeter-wave (mmWave) bands.
