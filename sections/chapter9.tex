\chapter{Vehicular Networks}
\label{sec:chapter9}

This chapter focuses on Vehicular Ad-hoc Networks (VANETs) and cellular vehicle-to-everything (C-V2X) architectures.

\section{Overview of Contributions}
Contributions from researchers under the Mobile Computing Lab include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Dhananjay Kumar:} Routing protocols and dynamic access schemes in high-mobility topologies.
    \item \textbf{Indulekha:} Safety message dissemination and resource allocation in V2X networks.
\end{itemize}

\section{Mobility Challenges & Safety Message Dissemination}
We review dynamic MAC modifications and low-latency schemes needed to handle highly dynamic network structures, Doppler shifts, and strict safety latency requirements.
