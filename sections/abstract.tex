Wireless networks have become a part and parcel of our every day life. Wireless networks are crucial for modern connectivity, enabling mobility,  productivity, entertainment, and seamless communication without the limitations of physical cables.They come in various forms, meeting different use case scenarios such as 5G/6G cellular networks, Wireless local area networks for home and office connectivity, body area and personal area networks for small range, sensor area networks, Internet of things, machine to machine communication networks etc.
