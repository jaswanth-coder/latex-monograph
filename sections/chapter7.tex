\chapter{Heterogeneous Networks (HetNets)}
\label{sec:chapter7}

This chapter details structural and coordination challenges in Heterogeneous Networks (HetNets) consisting of macrocells, microcells, picocells, and femtocells.

\section{Overview of Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Anusree:} Internetworking and mobile data offloading strategies.
\end{itemize}

\section{Mobile Data Offloading \& Internetworking}
We review techniques to offload cellular network traffic onto local networks (such as Wi-Fi) to mitigate congestion, examining traffic steering, vertical handoffs, and resource optimization.
